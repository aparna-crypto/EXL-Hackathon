## 4. Methodology (P3)

*Confidence: high*

Weight-of-evidence (WoE) binning followed by logistic regression, scaled to points. (1) Numeric variables with more than 10 distinct values are cut into 5 quantile bins (type 7, breaks from training data, outer breaks -Inf/Inf); other variables are categorical with one bin per level. (2) WoE per bin = ln(((n_good+0.5)/total_good)/((n_bad+0.5)/total_bad)); IV computed with the same smoothing. (3) Variables with IV >= 0.02 are retained (14 selected; all non-excluded candidates appear to pass or the selected list is as shown in metrics.json). (4) Logistic regression (glm, binomial) of bad on the WoE-transformed variables, no regularisation or stepwise selection. (5) Scaling: factor = PDO/ln(2), offset = 600 - factor*ln(50), score = offset + factor*ln((1-PD)/PD), i.e. 600 points at good:bad odds 50:1 with PDO 20. All fitted WoE coefficients are negative except woe_job (+0.657, std error 0.670, not significant), which has a counter-intuitive sign for a WoE-transformed variable and indicates multicollinearity or instability. Several coefficients (credit_amount, other_debtors, property, age, housing, job) have standard errors comparable to or larger than the estimate.
