Executive summary

This re-validation, triggered by two consecutive amber monitoring months, rates the Retail Application Scorecard (RCS-APP-02) red overall, driven entirely by calibration. The model should not be relied on for PD-level decisions until it is recalibrated and the drift is investigated.

Limitations of this validation

  • The evaluation sample is a monitoring batch with synthetic drift injected, so results demonstrate the validation process but may not represent live portfolio behaviour.
  • The underlying data (German Credit) has no application date, so the out-of-time back-test uses record-id order as a synthetic time ordering and is not a true out-of-time test.
  • The test sample is small (about 1,000 records), which widens the uncertainty around all estimates, including the AUC interval.
  • Fairness testing covers only the attributes available (sex, age group, foreign worker); the foreign worker group was too small to rate, and no other protected characteristics were assessed.
  • Fairness results depend on the single cut-off of 496.5 and may differ at other cut-offs or under a different decision rule.
