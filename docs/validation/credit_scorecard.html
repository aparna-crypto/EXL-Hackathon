Findings and recommendations

mediumstabilityThe worst synthetic out-of-time tranche shows a score PSI of 0.130, above the green threshold, while train-to-test PSI is green. The tranches are an id-ordered split rather than true time periods, so the drift signal cannot be attributed to genuine temporal change.Run a true out-of-time back-test on dated application data before or shortly after deployment, and set up periodic score PSI and CSI monitoring with escalation triggers in the ongoing monitoring plan.
mediumfairnessThe age-group equalized odds difference is 0.132, above the green limit, and age is a direct model input. The foreign worker comparison could not be rated because one group has only 9 records.Model Development should investigate which age bands drive the gap, assess whether the age bins are justified by risk evidence, and test alternatives such as coarser banding or constraints. Fairness testing for foreign worker status should be repeated on a larger sample or with pooled data, and the age result should be tracked in ongoing monitoring.

Limitations of this validation

  • Validation uses German Credit (UCI) with a single fixed 700/300 split, so the test sample of 300 records gives wide uncertainty on discrimination metrics.
  • No application date exists, so the out-of-time back-test is a synthetic ordering by record id and does not demonstrate performance or stability through time.
  • The foreign worker group (A202) is too small to assess, so fairness for that attribute remains untested.
  • Fairness testing uses a single decision rule (cut-off 496.5) and a single favourable outcome definition; other cut-offs and intersectional groups were not assessed.
  • The data is a public benchmark rather than the bank's own portfolio, so representativeness of the bank's applicant population, economic conditions and reject-inference effects were not assessed.
