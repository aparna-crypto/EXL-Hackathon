RCS-APP-02 Retail Application Scorecard - Methodology note (version 1.0, June 2019)

The scorecard uses four coarse-classed characteristics: checking account status, loan
duration, credit history and savings balance. Each characteristic is grouped into 4 bins.
The model is a logistic regression on dummy-coded bins. Scores are scaled to 500 points
at odds 20:1 with 25 points to double the odds.

Last independent validation: March 2021 (internal model validation team).
