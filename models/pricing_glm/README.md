# PRC-MTPL-03 Motor TPL claim frequency GLM

*Last updated: 2024-09 by Personal Lines Pricing.*

Poisson GLM with log link and log(exposure) offset, fitted on policy-level motor third-party
liability data (public freMTPL2 dataset as a stand-in). Rating factors: vehicle power, vehicle
age band, driver age band (base 31-40), bonus-malus level (capped at 150), log population
density, area, vehicle brand, fuel type and region. Exposure is capped at one year and claim
counts at four.

Owner: Personal Lines Pricing. Last validation: 2024-11 (annual pricing review).
